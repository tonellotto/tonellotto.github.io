I am an Associate Professor at the Information Engineering Department of the University of Pisa. I received the PhD degree from the University of Pisa and the Technical University of Dortmund, in 2008. Since 2023, I am a distinguihed member of ACM.<br>
My research interests mainly focus on efficiency in Web search engines, neural information retrieval, cloud computing and machine learning.<br><br>
I'm passionate about information retrieval, language models, and coding. Whether optimizing search algorithms or exploring the depths of natural language processing, I love tackling complex challenges with code. When I’m not immersed in teaching and research, you’ll find me weaving intricate stories and unforgettable adventures in the world of Dungeons & Dragons.<br>
